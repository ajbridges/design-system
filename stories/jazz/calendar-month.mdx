# Calendar month

## Overview

One month of sets. A day list on a phone, a seven-column month on a wide screen.

## When to use

Use it to browse which nights have music.

## When not to use

Do not use it to pick an arbitrary date in a form. That is the calendar control.

## Anatomy

Month name, previous and next, then either grouped days or a grid. Each set can show a ticket status. Today uses the accent.

## Variants and states

<Canvas of={Stories.November} />

<Canvas of={Stories.EmptyMonth} />

An empty month says there are no sets. Selecting a day marks it.

## Content guidelines

Set titles stay in sentence case. Keep them short enough to truncate in a cell.

## Accessibility

Previous and next are named buttons. Day cells and list rows are buttons. The month name is a heading.

## Do

Pass the sets for that month. Let the layout switch at the md breakpoint.

## Don't

Do not show both the day list and the grid at once. Do not use uppercase weekday headings.
