# Follow button

## Overview

Follow or unfollow an artist or a room. The label changes with the state.

## When to use

Use it when following changes what the person will see later.

## When not to use

Do not use it to like one set. That is the like button.

## Anatomy

A pill, at least 44px tall. Plus and the word Follow, or a check and the word Following.

## Variants and states

<Canvas of={Stories.Follow} />

<Canvas of={Stories.Following} />

## Content guidelines

Keep the words Follow and Following. Put the name in the accessible label, not necessarily in the visible word.

## Accessibility

`aria-pressed` matches the state. The name includes the artist or the room, such as Follow SFJAZZ.

## Do

Give it a name so the person knows who they are following.

## Don't

Do not shrink it below 44px. Do not use uppercase.
