# Ticket status pill

## Overview

The state of tickets for a set: on sale, few left, sold out, no cover, or cancelled.

## When to use

Use it wherever a set shows whether you can still get in.

## When not to use

Do not use it for account or payment status. Use a badge for those.

## Anatomy

A pill with a word. Color follows the meaning. On sale uses the live accent. Few left is a warning. Sold out is neutral. No cover is success. Cancelled is destructive.

## Variants and states

<Canvas of={Stories.All} />

## Content guidelines

The words are fixed and in sentence case. Do not rename them per screen.

## Accessibility

The text is the status. Color is extra. The pill is not a button.

## Do

Show one status per set.

## Don't

Do not rely on the color alone. Do not invent a sixth status in the app.
