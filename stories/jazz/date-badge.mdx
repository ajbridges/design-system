# Date badge

## Overview

A small stack of month and day, in the accent color, at least 44px.

## When to use

Use it on a poster or a compact row when the day should be seen before the title.

## When not to use

Do not use it for a full sentence date in a paragraph. Write that as text.

## Anatomy

Month abbreviation, then the day number, on a dark chip.

## Variants and states

<Canvas of={Stories.Friday} />

There is one shape. The date is the only content.

## Content guidelines

Let the component format the month. Do not uppercase it yourself.

## Accessibility

The accessible name is the full date, such as November 27, 2026. The visual month and day are the same information, shortened.

## Do

Put one badge on a card.

## Don't

Do not use it as a button. Do not pair it with a second copy of the same date in the same card.
